The heat scale

Three levels of warm

Our studios are warmed by far-infrared panels, so the heat feels like sun on skin rather than a blast of hot air. Look for the dots on every class.

85–90°F

Warm

Gentle, enveloping warmth for slow practice and recovery. Ideal for your first visit or a rest day.

90–95°F

Hot

A steady sweat that keeps muscles supple through precise, controlled work.

95–100°F

Into the red

Our warmest rooms for flowing, full-body practice. Hydrate well and take rest whenever you need it.

The classes

Six ways in

Heated vinyasa

Wärme Flow

60 min95–100°FAll levelsStudio I

Our foundation class. A breath-led vinyasa that moves through sun salutations, standing sequences and a long, slow cool-down — set to a curated soundtrack, under infrared panels that warm you from the first breath.

What to expect

Strength, mobility and a deep, honest sweat. Clear cues, hands-on assists by consent and plenty of options to go further or rest.

Best for

Everyone. If you’re new to Wärme — or to yoga — this is the place to start.

Into the Red

75 min100°FIntermediateStudio I

The fullest expression of the Wärme method. Longer holds, deeper backbends and hip openers, playful arm-balance work — then ten minutes of complete stillness to let the heat settle in.

What to expect

Our warmest, longest class. Advanced options are offered, never expected. Arrive hydrated.

Regular practitioners ready for more time on the mat and a slower, deeper burn.

Yoga + strength

Wärme Sculpt

50 min95°FAll levelsStudio II

Heated yoga woven with light hand weights and strength intervals. Low impact, high sweat, set to a rhythm that keeps you moving from the first minute to the last.

What to expect

1–3 lb weights, sculpting sequences and a short core finisher. Weights are provided.

Best for

Anyone who wants yoga’s mobility with a little more muscle — and a playlist to match.

Infrared mat

Hot Pilates

50 min90–95°FAll levelsStudio II

Precise, core-led mat pilates in infrared warmth. Small movements, big focus — with bands and balls to find the muscles you didn’t know you had.

Controlled, slow-tempo work for the deep core, glutes and posture. Less sweat, more shake.

Warm yin

Slow Burn

Warm yin and breathwork. Long, supported floor holds that ease into the fascia, with guided breathing to bring your nervous system all the way down.

What to expect

Few poses, held for three to five minutes, with bolsters and blankets. Quiet, candle-lit and unhurried.

Evenings, rest days and anyone who finds stillness harder than sweat.

Your first class

What to know
before you glow

Heat is welcoming, but it asks a little of you. Here’s how to arrive ready — and leave feeling like you’ve been somewhere.

Before

Hydrate & arrive early

Drink water through the day and eat lightly two hours before class. Arrive 15 minutes early so we can show you around, set your mat and answer any questions.

Bring

Just yourself & a bottle

Wear light, close-fitting clothes you’re happy to sweat in. We provide mats, two towels, lockers, showers and bath amenities — and electrolytes at the desk.

During

Rest is always allowed

Child’s pose is a full part of the practice here. Let your teacher know about injuries, pregnancy or anything else they should know before class begins.

After

Cool down slowly

Rinse off in the rain showers, then sit with a cup of tea in the lounge. There’s no rush to get back out into the world.

Questions

Good to know

Do I need yoga experience?

Not at all. Every class is taught for mixed levels. If you’re brand new, Wärme Flow, Slow Burn or Red Light Restore are lovely places to begin.

What is infrared heat — and how is it different?

Traditional hot studios heat the air with forced-air systems. Far-infrared panels warm people and surfaces directly, the way sunlight does. Most people find the heat feels gentler, more even and easier to breathe in.

What if the heat feels like too much?

Rest in child’s pose or lie down — you can always step out to cool off. Teachers are trained to watch for heat stress, and the heat can be dialled down in our warm classes.

Can I practise while pregnant or with a health condition?

Please speak with your healthcare provider before practising in heat, and let your teacher know before class. Our warm-level classes may be a gentler option, but your provider’s guidance comes first.

Do I need to bring a mat or towel?

No. Mats and two towels are included with every class and every membership. Just bring a water bottle — or pick up a Wärme bottle at the desk.

What is the cancellation policy?

Cancel up to 12 hours before class at no charge. Later cancellations and no-shows forfeit the class credit, or incur a $15 fee for unlimited members.
