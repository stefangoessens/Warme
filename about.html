About Wärme

Warmth,
as a practice

A hot yoga studio that feels less like a gym and more like somewhere you belong.

Our story

Why warmth

Wärme — German for warmth — began with a simple question: why do so many hot studios feel cold?

Fluorescent lights, crowded rooms, a rush to get in and out. We loved what heat did for our practice, but we wanted a place that felt as good as the class itself — somewhere you could arrive early, stay late and feel looked after from the door to the shower.

So we built it in West Palm Beach, a city that already lives in the warmth. Infrared heat instead of forced air. Arches, plaster and stone instead of mirrors and rubber. Teachers trained to read a heated room. And a lounge, because the best part of a practice is often the quiet that comes after.

Wärme is our answer: hot yoga as a ritual of care. Come as you are. Leave a little warmer.

The mark

Two points
of light

Our mark is an open arch beneath two glowing dots. It’s the umlaut in Wärme, the doorways that shape our studio, and the first warm light you see when you walk in from the street.

The arch has no crossbar on purpose: it’s an opening, not a wall. A shelter you can step into, a peak you can climb — and always, a way back out into the world, a little softer.

What we believe

Four warm truths

01

Warmth before intensity

Heat should invite you in, not prove something. Effort is welcome; ego isn’t required.

02

Ritual over routine

From the scent at the door to the tea after class, every detail is designed to help you arrive.

03

Crafted, not cranked

Thoughtful sequencing and real teaching — not just a hot room and a loud playlist.

04

Hosted, not handled

We know your name, your injuries and your favourite spot in the room. That’s the point.

The space

A walk through Wärme

Plaster, stone, brass and a red glow — every room is designed to warm the senses before the heat ever touches your skin.

01 — The arrival

Reception

A curved oxblood desk, a glowing arch and fresh towels stacked in the alcove. Check in, slow down, and let us take it from here.

02 — The changing rooms

Lockers & showers

Arched private lockers, a warm stone bench and rain showers stocked with bath amenities. Everything you need to walk out ready for the day.

03 — The cool-down

The lounge

Boucle and velvet, a travertine table, loose-leaf tea and fresh citrus. Stay as long as you like — this is where the community happens.

Our teachers

Trained for the heat

Every Wärme teacher holds a 200-hour or higher yoga or pilates certification — and then trains with us.

  • 50-hour Wärme Heat Method training in sequencing, cueing and hands-on assists
  • Heat-safety, hydration and CPR/First Aid certification
  • Ongoing mentorship, workshops and peer observation
  • A shared promise: every student seen, every level held
