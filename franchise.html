Ideal partners

Who we’re
looking for

Wellness experience helps, but it isn’t required. What matters most is how you lead, how you host and how much you care about your community.

  • A genuine passion for wellness, hospitality and design
  • Business, management or multi-unit operating experience
  • Access to the capital and liquidity outlined in our FDD
  • Deep roots in — and a network within — your local community

  • Owner-operators and investor-operators with a strong studio manager welcome

Franchise questions

Asked & answered

Do I need to be a yoga teacher or studio owner?

No. Many great operators come from hospitality, retail or general business. We train your team on the method — you bring leadership and community.

Do I need to run the studio day to day?

We welcome both owner-operators and investor-operators who hire a strong full-time studio manager. We’ll discuss what fits your goals on our discovery call.

What does it cost to open a Wärme?

Investment ranges, franchise fees and ongoing royalties are detailed in Items 5, 6 and 7 of our Franchise Disclosure Document, which we provide to qualified candidates after an initial call.

How long does it take to open?

Timelines depend on site selection, permitting and construction in your market. We’ll walk you through a typical project schedule during discovery.

Can I open more than one studio?

Yes. Multi-unit and area-developer agreements are available in select territories for qualified partners.

This information is not intended as an offer to sell, or the solicitation of an offer to buy, a franchise. An offer can only be made in states where, if required, we have registered and delivered a Franchise Disclosure Document. Certain states, including California, Hawaii, Illinois, Indiana, Maryland, Michigan, Minnesota, New York, North Dakota, Rhode Island, South Dakota, Virginia, Washington and Wisconsin, regulate the offer and sale of franchises. We will not offer or sell franchises in those states until we have registered (or are exempt) and delivered the FDD to you in compliance with applicable law.
