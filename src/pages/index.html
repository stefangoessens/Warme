The Wärme method

Heat. Move.
Restore.

Three simple ideas, practised with care. Every class is designed around how the body responds to warmth — and what it needs afterwards.

01 — Heat

Infrared, not forced air

Far-infrared panels warm you and the room’s surfaces instead of blasting hot air. The heat feels deep and even, the air stays breathable, and your muscles soften from the first sun salutation.

02 — Move

Breath-led classes

Vinyasa, sculpt and pilates sequenced by teachers trained in our heat method — challenging when you want it, always with room to rest. Every level is welcome in every room.

03 — Restore

Red light & the lounge

Close the loop with Red Light Restore, a rain shower and a cup of tea in the lounge. Cooling down is part of the practice here — not something you rush on the way to the car.
