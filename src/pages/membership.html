Membership questions

The fine print

What does “founding rate” mean?

The first 150 Unlimited members pay $199 a month instead of $229 — and keep that rate for as long as their membership stays active, including freezes.

Can I freeze my membership?

Yes. Freeze for up to two months per year for $15 a month — perfect for travel season. Medical freezes are free with a note from your provider.

How do I cancel?

After your initial three-month term, email us or speak to the front desk with 30 days’ notice. No forms, no awkward conversations.

Can I switch plans?

Upgrade anytime and the change takes effect immediately. Downgrades take effect at your next billing date.

How do guest passes work?

Bring a friend who hasn’t visited before. Guests receive the full Wärme experience — towels, mats, showers and a cup of tea after.

What is the late-cancel policy?

Cancel at least 12 hours before class. Late cancels and no-shows forfeit the class credit, or incur a $15 fee on Unlimited.
